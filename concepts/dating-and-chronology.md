# Dating and Chronology

The Great Pyramid is dated to ~2600 BC by two approaches: (1) indirect attribution to [[khufu]] and his chronological age based on archaeological and textual evidence; (2) direct radiocarbon dating of organic material in mortar.

## Historical Chronology

Egyptian chronology is fundamentally a POLITICAL chronology — derived from king lists, reign lengths, and genealogies — and is largely independent of radiocarbon dating, stratigraphy, or material culture. When radiocarbon contradicts the chronology, the chronology is assumed correct and the radiocarbon is "explained" by the old wood problem. This is a methodological choice, not a proven fact.

Modern estimates of Khufu's first regnal year range from 2613 BC (Ramsey et al. 2010) to 2480 BC (Spence 2000). The majority fall between 2700-2500 BC.

## Radiocarbon Dating

The 1984 and 1995 Koch Pyramids Radiocarbon Project took 46 mortar samples. Results calibrated to 2871-2604 BC — 100-300 years older than the historical chronology. The "old wood problem" is the standard explanation: organic material in mortar (ash from fires) came from old wood, not from the time of construction.

The cedar plank from the Queen's Chamber air shaft, dated in 2020 to 3341-3094 BC, is 500+ years older than Khufu — explained as "recycled wood from the center of a long-lived tree." Each anomaly is explained away individually, but the pattern of consistently older dates deserves more scrutiny.

Reanalysis using the "younger samples" gave 2620-2484 BC, which overlaps the historical chronology — but cherry-picking the youngest samples is methodologically questionable.

## Astronomical Alignment Dating

Kate Spence (2000) proposed that the alignment drift across different pyramids tracks the precessional movement of stars. This could both date the pyramids and explain the small but varying alignment errors. Requires assuming a specific stellar alignment method. Error bars: ±50-100 years.

## Unresolved Anomalies

- The cedar plank (3341-3094 BC) — if not recycled wood, it could indicate the shafts or chamber predate Khufu.
- The consistent 100-300 year offset in mortar radiocarbon vs the political chronology.
- No OSL (Optically Stimulated Luminescence) dating has been done on the limestone blocks themselves.

## Tests That Could Resolve Dating

- **AMS dating** of additional organic samples from core mortar, relieving chambers, and the [[big-void]].
- **OSL dating** of core blocks: measures time since mineral grain was last exposed to sunlight → gives quarrying date. Never applied to the Great Pyramid.
- **Dendrochronological cross-matching** of the cedar plank with Levantine tree-ring sequences.

## Implications

If OSL dates of core blocks predate Khufu significantly, this supports the hypothesis that the structure (or part of it) predates the 4th Dynasty. If [[pyramid-evolution-timeline]] shows technology developing across reigns, the construction timeline may span 50+ years across two reigns (Snefru → Khufu).
