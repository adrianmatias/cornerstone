# Shaft Alignments

The "air shafts" from the King's and Queen's Chambers are ~20 cm² cross-section, cut through up to 70 m of masonry with bends and angle changes. Their precision of construction exceeds any functional ventilation need.

## Stellar Targets

Calculated by Virginia Trimble (1964), confirmed by Rudolf [[gantenbrink]]'s Upuaut robot measurements (1993):

- King's Chamber south → Orion's Belt (Alnitak)
- King's Chamber north → Thuban (Polaris predecessor, the pole star of the era)
- Queen's Chamber south → Sirius
- Queen's Chamber north → Kochab

## Implications

If real, these alignments mean the internal geometry was designed to channel starlight — or the concept of starlight — into the chambers. This is consistent with:
- The Egyptian "opening of the mouth" ritual
- The stellar afterlife theology of the Old Kingdom (the king ascending to circumpolar stars)

The engineering implication: the shafts were planned from the beginning of construction (they run through 60-70 m of masonry with precise angular control), meaning the pyramid was not just a tomb but a calibrated astronomical instrument. See [[purpose-and-function]].

## The "Door" in the Queen's Chamber Shaft

The Upuaut robot (1993, [[gantenbrink]]) explored the southern shaft of the Queen's Chamber and found a "door" with copper fittings at ~65 m. A 2002 National Geographic expedition drilled through this door and found a small empty space and another blocking stone. Drilling through the second blocking stone has been blocked by bureaucracy for 20+ years. See [[internal-structure]].

## Robert Bauval's Orion Correlation Theory

The theory that the Giza pyramids map the belt stars of Orion is dismissed by mainstream Egyptologists but the shaft alignments themselves are measured facts. The stellar targets are consistent with Old Kingdom afterlife theology.
