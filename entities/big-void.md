# ScanPyramids Big Void (SP-BV)

A previously unknown void above the Grand Gallery of the Great Pyramid, discovered by [[scanpyramids]] and published in Nature on November 2, 2017.

## Characteristics

- Length: ≥30 m (exact extent unknown)
- Cross-section: similar to the Grand Gallery
- Location: above the Grand Gallery, within the pyramid's masonry
- Observed from 3 independent viewpoints (2 in the Queen's Chamber, 1 outside the North Face) using 3 different muon detection techniques

## Unresolved Questions

- **Shape**: Horizontal (like a second Grand Gallery), inclined (like an internal ramp segment), or a chamber? Muon tomography has limited angular resolution. More detector positions needed.
- **Function**: Structural relief? Part of an internal ramp system? A sealed chamber containing construction records, artifacts, or the actual burial?

## Tests to Resolve

- Deploy muon detectors inside the Grand Gallery at multiple heights and along its full length, plus in the King's Chamber and relieving chambers.
- Deploy a micro-drone through a bore hole into the Big Void from the Grand Gallery ceiling.
- Both proposed but not executed due to conservation concerns.

## Implications

If the Big Void is a second, parallel gallery, it could be part of an internal ramp system (see [[houdin-internal-ramp]]) or a second load-relieving structure. If a sealed chamber, it could contain the most significant archaeological discovery in the pyramid since the 19th century.
