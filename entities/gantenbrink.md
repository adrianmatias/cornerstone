# Rudolf Gantenbrink

German robotics engineer who designed and operated the Upuaut robot (1993) to explore the air shafts of the Great Pyramid.

## Upuaut Mission (1993)

- Explored the southern shaft of the Queen's Chamber
- Found a "door" (blocking stone) with copper fittings at ~65 m
- The copper fittings suggested the door was deliberately placed and sealed
- Measurements of shaft angles confirmed the [[shaft-alignments]] calculated by Virginia Trimble (1964)

## Legacy

- The 2002 National Geographic expedition drilled through the first "door" and found a small empty space and another blocking stone. The second blocking stone has not been penetrated.
- Gantenbrink's precise measurements of the shaft angles enabled the stellar alignment analysis that underpins the astronomical interpretation of the pyramid's internal geometry.
