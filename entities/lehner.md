# Mark Lehner

American Egyptologist, director of the Giza Plateau Mapping Project. One of the leading authorities on the Great Pyramid and the Giza complex.

## Key Contributions

- Discovered workers' cemeteries with Zahi Hawass (1990), changing the paradigm from slave labor to paid/skilled workforce.
- Proposed the combined ramp + lever construction model (consensus position): ramps for lower courses, levers for top 3-4% of material.
- Conducted the Nova pyramid-building experiment (1992) with stonemason Roger Hopkins, testing construction techniques at small scale.
- Co-authored "The Red Sea Scrolls" with Pierre Tallet, documenting the [[diary-of-merer]].
- Estimated peak workforce of 40,000, average 14,567 (with Daniel, Mann, Johnson & Mendenhall).
- In earlier work, proposed the initiation interpretation of the internal chambers — the journey through the pyramid as a ritual passage. See [[occult-and-hermetic-traditions]].

## Position

- Supports the tomb interpretation but acknowledges the internal complexity.
- Skeptical of the [[houdin-internal-ramp]] and [[geopolymer-hypothesis]].
- The spiral external ramp he proposed would have covered the casing during construction — a known weakness.
