# North Face Corridor (SP-NFC)

A corridor-shaped void behind the chevrons above the original entrance to the Great Pyramid, discovered by [[scanpyramids]].

## Characteristics

- Location: 17-23 m above ground level, 0.7-2 m from the north face
- Shape: corridor-like, possibly horizontal or sloping upwards
- Published in Nature Communications, March 2023
- Confirmed by endoscopic inspection — a real void

## Context

The void was first investigated after thermal anomalies were observed on the north face during infrared thermography. Muon emulsion plates were positioned in the Descending Corridor and Al-Ma'mun's tunnel to confirm and refine the discovery.

## Significance

The NFC is the first void in the Great Pyramid to be both confirmed by muon tomography AND visually inspected with an endoscope. Its corridor shape and location behind the entrance chevrons suggest it may be part of the original construction design, possibly related to load distribution or an unexplored access route.
