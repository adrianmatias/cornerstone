# Extinct Nile Branch (Ahramat Branch)

A now-disappeared branch of the Nile that once flowed near the Giza pyramids, confirmed by a May 2024 study.

## Discovery

- Mapped using satellite imaging and sediment core analysis
- 64 km (40 mi) long, ~0.5 km (0.31 mi) wide, depth ≥25 m (82 ft)
- Likely disappeared due to drought and desertification
- Previously proposed by Hader Sheisha (Aix-Marseille University, 2012)

## Significance

This waterway was crucial for transporting materials and labor to the pyramid construction site. The Nile's present waterways have receded too far from the pyramid sites to be of use. The Ahramat Branch explains how the [[diary-of-merer]] documents efficient boat transport of Tura limestone to the Giza harbour (She-Khufu).
